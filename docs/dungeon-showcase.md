# Dungeon and late fixtures

Choose **Test seeds → Dungeon & late fixtures · DNGS80EC → New Run**. The direct Quest playtest URL is [localhost:4260/?seed=DNGS80EC&playtest&difficulty=casual](http://localhost:4260/?seed=DNGS80EC&playtest&difficulty=casual). The showcase has its own save and workshop. To resume it, open [its title screen](http://localhost:4260/?seed=DNGS80EC&title&playtest) and choose **Continue**.

This is a playable combat and puzzle route. Room numbers are visible from the radial menu. In Lower Works room 0, use the stair door marked **Dungeon · Floor 2**. From the Upper Works landing, follow the **Stone dungeon** sign through the adjacent corridor and continue to rooms 3 and 4. Use the Astral Lift in Upper Works room 0 to reach the Conservatory. Regular encounters and puzzle locks work as in a normal run.
