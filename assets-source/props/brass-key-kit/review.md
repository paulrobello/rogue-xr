# Brass coffer key and wall rack

The 22 cm key hangs from the Upper Works rack. Its open bow, three offset teeth and turned collar distinguish it from the winding key. The rack and matching padlock badge use the same inlaid three-bar mark.

The exported kit contains 13,212 triangles and four materials. Each material embeds its 256-pixel base color, metallic-roughness and normal maps. The [manifest](manifest.json) records the source, generator and GLB hashes. The independent review accepted GLB `f351ca649ba737252cf9d13211d6267f3955d821c88b7bc534c6503f45aee52b` for game integration after the artist joined the collar and decorative beads and seated the bow behind the hook lip. [Key close-up](staging/key-close.png) · [Hung key](staging/rack-hung.png) · [Hook contact](staging/rack-oblique.png) · [Empty rack](staging/rack-empty.png)

The rack back lies at glTF z = 0 with +Z facing the room. The key uses identity rotation as a child of `BrassKeySocket` at (0, −0.052, 0.063) m in rack space. The teeth hang below the plaque. The key, rack and lock badge share the loaded GLB materials at runtime.
