# Clockmaker’s cabinet and instruments

The [manifest](manifest.json) binds the editable Blender source, generator and runtime export. Independent visual review accepted the kit for integration. The instrument frame leaves a 1.36 × 0.67 m slate opening. Each orrery arm rotates about the common forward axis. The ivory crescent, ember sun and ringed blue planet have distinct silhouettes; the separate controls on the lower rail remain usable when the arms align.

The [cabinet](staging/review-front.png) leaves its parchment blank for the run’s gear or astronomical chart. The [drawer](staging/review-drawer-open.png) advances 18 cm and carries its card socket. The runtime supplies the seeded card’s reward artwork and raises it from the tray after the drawer opens. Owner art and seated-comfort approval remain pending.
