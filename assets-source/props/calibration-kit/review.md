# Clockmaker’s cabinet and instruments

The instrument frame has a 1.36 × 0.67 m slate opening. Its crescent moon, ember sun and ringed planet rotate on a common shaft above three rows of brass bearing shoes. The control knobs occupy the right side of the case. The left drive carries the broken collar and accepts the replacement coupling. The manifest records the editable Blender source, generator, runtime export and nine walnut and brass texture inputs. [Orrery](staging/review-orrery.png) · [Asset manifest](manifest.json)

The cabinet displays the run’s gear or astronomical chart and mounts the orrery spare in a separate cradle. Its drawer advances 18 cm and the card rises from the tray. [Cabinet](staging/review-cabinet.png) · [Open drawer](staging/review-drawer-open.png)

The balance has an independently pivoted beam, suspended pans, two removable weights, a reference weight and a chain with an open terminal hook. The game keeps the pans level as the beam tilts, carries a selected weight through an arc, and lets the beam settle as the weight lands. [Balance](staging/review-balance.png)

Independent Astra Extra High review of the production build on Chromium with ANGLE Metal scored the orrery 85/100, the cabinet 82/100 and the balance 84/100. The controller-emulated WIND0001 run collected, installed and persisted the coupling, then wound the instrument and claimed the reward after visiting both floors. The MAPTEST2 motion capture showed a weight travel from its dock to a pan and then to the terminal hook. Quest footage and owner review determine final interaction and seated-comfort acceptance.
