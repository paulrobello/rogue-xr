# Keeper’s ledger and recoveries

The editable Blender source, generator and runtime GLB hashes are in the [manifest](manifest.json). The exported and reimported kit has 26,953 triangles, with embedded original color, roughness and normal maps. The [open-letter backing](staging/review-letter-open.png) is blank so the live order text has a clear surface. A separate red wax stamp signs the work order.

Independent visual review accepted the revised [drawstring purse](staging/review-purse-close.png) for integration. The flat spiral, wound spool and bowed escapement have different profiles at hand range. Pickup perches tilt their mounting plates upward and toward a seated viewer. Owner visual and comfort approval remain open.
