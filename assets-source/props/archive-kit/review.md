# Archive art

The independent visual review accepted export `a8da92c89a92ff8bd001ef275e902c8d7dabf13860473ed3599e96ce4f28bb90` for integration. The Blender source, generator and runtime GLB hashes are in the [manifest](manifest.json). Each room instantiates its chosen variant.

The [blank masonry](staging/review-ink-before.png) and [developed inscription](staging/review-ink-after.png) share the same leaves. The lines and jewel are independent controls. The [draft fan](staging/review-wind.png) rotates around its exported shaft. The [mirror](staging/review-mirror.png) uses tarnished PBR silver and shows the physical room beyond as the coating clears. The [rubbing](staging/review-rubbing-close.png), wind chart and hand mirror have sockets for the seed-specific ingredient glyph. The finer points of the inscription are intended for close inspection.

The kit uses 28,097 triangles in total. The materials carry embedded color, roughness and normal maps. Owner art and immersive comfort review remain open.
